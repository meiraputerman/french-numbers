# À l'oreille

Practise French numbers by ear. The game says a number in French and you type it in digits as fast as you can. The quicker you answer, the more points you get.

**Play:** https://meiraputerman.github.io/a-l-oreille/

## What you can practise

| Mode | What you hear |
|---|---|
| 0 to 60, 0 to 99, 60 to 99 | Whole numbers, including soixante-dix, quatre-vingts and quatre-vingt-dix |
| Hundreds | 100 to 999, often ending in the 70s, 80s or 90s |
| Thousands | 1 000 to 999 999 |
| Years | "en mille six cent quatre-vingt-cinq" (type 1685) |
| Full dates | "le quatorze juillet mille sept cent quatre-vingt-neuf" (type 14/7/1789 or 14 juillet 1789) |
| Prices | "douze euros cinquante" (type 12,50 or 12.50) |
| Phone numbers | Ten digits said in pairs |
| Mix | A bit of everything |

## How to play

- Answer by typing, by picking from 3 to 6 choices, or by saying the number in English.
- A wrong answer doesn't give the number away: you hear it again and try another answer, but that number scores no points.
- Skip shows the answer and moves on.
- A fast first-try answer scores up to 100 points, less for each second you take and each time you tap Hear again, plus a streak bonus of up to 50.

## What you need

- Chrome, on Android or a computer. The numbers are spoken by your device's text-to-speech, so install a French voice if the accent sounds English.
- For spoken answers, allow the microphone when Chrome asks.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser, and settings and best scores are saved only there. If you answer by voice, Chrome may send the recording to Google's speech service to turn it into text.

## Files

`index.html` is the whole game: one file, no build step, no server.
